Our Trips

Three ways to run the river this season — pick the trip that matches your crew's experience and how much time you've got on the water.

Class III · Half-Day

Canyon Sprint

A fast-paced half-day run through the narrow canyon section, with eight named rapids packed into six miles. Ideal for first-timers who want real whitewater without an overnight commitment. Includes gear, a certified guide, and a riverside lunch.

Class IV · Full-Day

Thunder Gorge Run

Our most technical trip: a full day of continuous Class IV water through Thunder Gorge, including the notorious Widowmaker drop. Recommended for paddlers with prior rafting experience. Two guides per raft, safety kayak support included.

Class II · 2 Days

Overnight Wilderness Float

A relaxed two-day float built for families, with gentle Class II water by day and a riverside basecamp by night — tents, campfire dinner, and breakfast included. A great introduction to overnight river travel.
