Our Trips

Three ways to run the river this season — pick the trip that matches your crew's experience and how much time you've got on the water.

Class II · Half-Day

Half-Day Express

Our beginner-friendly intro trip: about three hours of easy, playful rapids, perfect for first-timers and families. Includes gear, a certified guide, and a quick safety briefing before you launch.

Class III–IV · Full-Day

Rapid Thunder

A full day of continuous, high-adrenaline whitewater for paddlers who want the real thing. Recommended for those with prior rafting experience. Two guides per raft, with safety kayak support included.

Class IV–V · 2 Days / Camping

Big Mallard

Our most extreme trip: two days of big, technical rapids with an overnight riverside camp — tents, campfire dinner, and breakfast included. Built for experienced paddlers chasing a real expedition.
